ABOUT OUR FARM

A Local Farm Built on Care & Dedication

Shaikh Poultry Farm is a local poultry farm located in Belwandi Bk, Chobhewadi. Our farm is focused on raising poultry birds with proper care and attention throughout their growth.

We believe that taking good care of birds during their growing period is an important part of responsible poultry farming. Our birds are raised in a dedicated farm environment and are made available for sale when they reach the appropriate age and size.

As a local poultry farm, we aim to serve customers and buyers from the surrounding areas. We take pride in being connected to our local community and continuing our work with dedication and responsibility.
